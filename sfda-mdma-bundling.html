🔬 SFDA MDMA Bundling Decision Flowchart 📋

IVD & Medical Devices | Regulatory Decision Framework

🇸🇦 Saudi Food & Drug Authority - Medical Device Market Authorization
flowchart TD
    START[Start: List all devices for MDMA application] --> Q1{Are the devices
IVDs or Non-IVDs?}
    
    Q1 -->|Non-IVD Medical Devices| Q2{Do they have the
same legal manufacturer?}
    Q1 -->|IVD Medical Devices| IVD_START
    
    Q2 -->|No| SEPARATE[Submit separate applications
Cannot bundle]
    Q2 -->|Yes| Q3{Do they form a
Procedure Pack?}
    
    Q3 -->|Yes| PACK[Procedure Pack Criteria
✔ Same intended use & specialty
✔ Same risk class of pack
✔ Max 5 TFs, Max 50 items
✔ Letter of agreement from each component mfr
✔ Submit TF for pack + each component]
    
    Q3 -->|No| Q4{Do they form a
System?
Used together for common purpose}
    
    Q4 -->|Yes| SYSTEM[System Criteria
✔ Same legal manufacturer
✔ Used in combination
✔ 1 system per application only
✔ Max 5 TFs, Max 50 items
✔ Highest risk class applies]
    
    Q4 -->|No| Q5{Are they
Surgical Instruments?}
    
    Q5 -->|Yes| SURGICAL[Surgical Instruments Family
✔ Same manufacturer, risk class, material, process
✔ Same FUNCTION cut/retract/grasp/dilate/cannulate/aspirate/suture
✔ NOT required: same intended use
✔ Max 5 TFs, Max 50 items]
    
    Q5 -->|No| Q6{Are they
Dental Instruments?}
    
    Q6 -->|Yes| DENTAL[Dental Instruments Family
✔ Same manufacturer, intended use, risk class
✔ Same SPECIALTY see 12 specialties
✔ Max 5 TFs, Max 50 items]
    
    Q6 -->|No| Q7{Medical Device Family
Same intended use, risk class,
GMDN code, design, material, process?}
    
    Q7 -->|Yes| FAMILY[Medical Device Family
✔ Max 5 TFs, Max 50 items
✔ Examples: catheters different lengths,
guide wires different tips, IOLs different diopters]
    
    Q7 -->|No| SEPARATE

    subgraph IVD_START [IVD Medical Devices]
        direction TB
        IVD_Q1{Are they Class A
specimen containers, dyes, stains,
culture media, reagents,
tissue processing, or cell culture supplies?}
        IVD_Q1 -->|Yes| IVD_EXEMPT[✅ Exempt bundled in one application
✔ Different intended uses allowed
✔ Not subject to same TF rule
✔ Max 50 items total
❌ Excludes blood collection devices, lab machinery, powered systems]
        IVD_Q1 -->|No| IVD_Q2{Same legal manufacturer?}
        IVD_Q2 -->|No| SEPARATE
        IVD_Q2 -->|Yes| IVD_Q3{Same risk class?}
        IVD_Q3 -->|No| SEPARATE
        IVD_Q3 -->|Yes| IVD_Q4{Same intended use/purpose
AND same principle of operation?
e.g., all PCR, all chromatography, all agglutination}
        IVD_Q4 -->|No| SEPARATE
        IVD_Q4 -->|Yes| IVD_Q5{Fall into ONE of 7 groupings?
1. Blood grouping
2. Tissue typing
3. Cancer markers
4. Genetic testing
5. Infections/immune status
6. Non-infectious pathologies
7. Controls without assigned value}
        IVD_Q5 -->|No| SEPARATE
        IVD_Q5 -->|Yes| IVD_BUNDLE[IVD Bundle
✔ Same clinical/performance evaluation data = 1 TF
✔ Max 5 TFs per application
✔ Max 50 items per application]
    end

    PACK --> CHECK_MAX{Total items ≤ 50?
Total TFs ≤ 5?
System: only 1 system?}
    SYSTEM --> CHECK_MAX
    SURGICAL --> CHECK_MAX
    DENTAL --> CHECK_MAX
    FAMILY --> CHECK_MAX
    IVD_EXEMPT --> CHECK_MAX
    IVD_BUNDLE --> CHECK_MAX
    
    CHECK_MAX -->|Yes| SUBMIT[✅ Proceed with single MDMA application]
    CHECK_MAX -->|No| SPLIT[❌ Split into multiple applications]
    
    SEPARATE --> END1[Submit separate applications]
    SUBMIT --> END2[Submit bundled application]
    SPLIT --> END3[Re-evaluate bundling strategy]
