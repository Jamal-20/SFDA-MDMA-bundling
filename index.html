flowchart TD
    START["🔬 START: List all devices for MDMA application"]
    
    START --> MAIN_DECISION{"What type
of device?"}
    
    MAIN_DECISION -->|"← Medical Devices →"| MEDICAL_START
    MAIN_DECISION -->|"← IVD Medical Devices →"| IVD_START
    
    %% ==================== MEDICAL DEVICES PATHWAY ====================
    subgraph MEDICAL_START [MEDICAL DEVICES]
        direction TB
        Q2{"Do they have the
same legal manufacturer?"}
    end
    
    Q2 -->|No| SEPARATE["Submit separate applications
Cannot bundle"]
    Q2 -->|Yes| Q3{"Do they form a
Procedure Pack?"}
    
    Q3 -->|Yes| PACK["📦 Procedure Pack Criteria
✔ Same intended use & specialty
✔ Same risk class of pack
✔ Max 5 TFs, Max 50 items
✔ Letter of agreement
✔ Submit TF for pack + each component"]
    
    Q3 -->|No| Q4{"Do they form a
System?
Used together for common purpose"}
    
    Q4 -->|Yes| SYSTEM["🖥️ System Criteria
✔ Same legal manufacturer
✔ Used in combination
✔ 1 system per application only
✔ Max 5 TFs, Max 50 items
✔ Highest risk class applies"]
    
    Q4 -->|No| Q5{"Are they
Surgical Instruments?"}
    
    Q5 -->|Yes| SURGICAL["🔪 Surgical Instruments Family
✔ Same manufacturer, risk class
✔ Same FUNCTION (cut, grasp, retract...)
✔ NOT required: same intended use
✔ Max 5 TFs, Max 50 items"]
    
    Q5 -->|No| Q6{"Are they
Dental Instruments?"}
    
    Q6 -->|Yes| DENTAL["🦷 Dental Instruments Family
✔ Same manufacturer, intended use
✔ Same SPECIALTY (12 specialties)
✔ Max 5 TFs, Max 50 items"]
    
    Q6 -->|No| Q7{"Same intended use, risk class,
GMDN code, design,
material, process?"}
    
    Q7 -->|Yes| FAMILY["🏷️ Medical Device Family
✔ Max 5 TFs, Max 50 items
✔ Examples: catheters, guide wires, IOLs"]
    
    Q7 -->|No| SEPARATE
    
    %% ==================== IVD MEDICAL DEVICES PATHWAY ====================
    subgraph IVD_START [IVD MEDICAL DEVICES]
        direction TB
        IVD_Q1{"Are they Class A exempt?
specimen containers, dyes,
stains, culture media,
reagents, tissue processing,
cell culture supplies?"}
    end
    
    IVD_Q1 -->|Yes| IVD_EXEMPT["🟢 Class A Exempt Bundle
✔ Different intended uses allowed
✔ Not subject to same TF rule
✔ Max 50 items total
❌ Excludes blood collection devices,
lab machinery, powered systems"]
    
    IVD_Q1 -->|No| IVD_Q2{"Same legal manufacturer?"}
    
    IVD_Q2 -->|No| SEPARATE
    IVD_Q2 -->|Yes| IVD_Q3{"Same risk class?"}
    
    IVD_Q3 -->|No| SEPARATE
    IVD_Q3 -->|Yes| IVD_Q4{"Same intended use/purpose
AND same principle of operation?
e.g., all PCR, all chromatography"}
    
    IVD_Q4 -->|No| SEPARATE
    IVD_Q4 -->|Yes| IVD_Q5{"Fall into ONE of 7 groupings?
1.Blood 2.Tissue 3.Cancer
4.Genetic 5.Infections
6.Non-infectious 7.Controls"}
    
    IVD_Q5 -->|No| SEPARATE
    IVD_Q5 -->|Yes| IVD_BUNDLE["🔬 Standard IVD Bundle
✔ Same clinical/performance data = 1 TF
✔ Max 5 TFs per application
✔ Max 50 items per application"]
    
    %% ==================== LIMITS CHECK ====================
    PACK --> CHECK_MAX
    SYSTEM --> CHECK_MAX
    SURGICAL --> CHECK_MAX
    DENTAL --> CHECK_MAX
    FAMILY --> CHECK_MAX
    IVD_EXEMPT --> CHECK_MAX
    IVD_BUNDLE --> CHECK_MAX
    
    CHECK_MAX{"Check limits:
Total items ≤ 50?
Total TFs ≤ 5?
System: only 1 system?"}
    
    CHECK_MAX -->|Yes| SUBMIT["✅ SUCCESS: Proceed with
single MDMA application"]
    CHECK_MAX -->|No| SPLIT["⚠️ WARNING: Split into
multiple applications"]
    
    SEPARATE --> END1["❌ Submit separate applications"]
    SUBMIT --> END2["✅ Submit bundled application"]
    SPLIT --> END3["⚠️ Re-evaluate bundling strategy"]
